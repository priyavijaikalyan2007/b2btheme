ChatDock

The conversation surface for a dynamic UI. Docks to the bottom and hovers over the canvas, which slides behind it — deliberately not a pinned sidebar, because the canvas is the work and chat is how you steer it.

Submit a message to add a turn. Click a turn to select it (the host would rewind the canvas); hover a turn and use ⑂ to branch. The two are separate controls because one navigates and the other creates.
