Relationship category badges

One colour for each of the eight relationship categories. Apply theme-ontology-category-<name>. It sets the background, the text and the border together, and changes with dark mode. Switch the theme in the header to see both.

Structural Ownership Strategic Knowledge Dependency Lifecycle Identity Custom

The tokens

A page that paints the colours itself reads --theme-ontology-category-<name>-bg, -fg and -border. Use the -fg token only on the matching -bg.

Inside a dark subtree

The badges below sit in a data-bs-theme="dark" container, so they take the dark colours on a light page too.
