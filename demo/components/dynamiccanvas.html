DynamicCanvas

The rendered surface of the Dynamic UI layer. It renders; the runtime decides — placement from the packer, mounting from lifecycle, delivery from the wiring engine, identity from the allowlisted registry.

This page shows the canvas in isolation. For the full experience — natural language, live bindings, the document inspector and the resolver explainer — see the Dynamic UI demo.

Drag a panel by its title bar to pin it to fixed coordinates.

Place annotation… arms the canvas: your next click lands a mark exactly where you point, and the canvas works out what you pointed at. Try it on a long document, then scroll — the mark travels with the content it marks, because what is recorded is a position in the content, not on the screen. Escape cancels.
