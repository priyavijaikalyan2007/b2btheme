DynamicCanvas

The rendered surface of the Dynamic UI layer. It renders; the runtime decides — placement from the packer, mounting from lifecycle, delivery from the wiring engine, identity from the allowlisted registry.

This page shows the canvas in isolation. For the full experience — natural language, live bindings, the document inspector and the resolver explainer — see the Dynamic UI demo.

Drag a panel by its title bar — it is promoted to fixed coordinates and the packer flows around it.
