Each row joins the row above with And or Or, and Not. A solid bracket is a group, with its own And or Or and Ungroup. A dashed bracket shows rows that the query reads together, since it reads and before or, and Make a group turns it solid. Check rows to group, ungroup, move or remove them from the toolbar. Drag a row or a group by its handle into, out of or between groups, or use Move up and Move down, or Alt with an arrow key. Alt+G groups and Alt+Shift+G ungroups. The search inside on the last row is read-only in this version. The API decides what a query means, so this page has no Parse or Print call. It shows the tree the builder reports.
